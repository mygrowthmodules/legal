Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026. Contact: hello@mygrowthmodules.com

This policy explains what MGM collects, why, and how you can delete it. MGM is a personal growth app (nutrition, bodyweight, steps, friends). It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

1. Who is responsible

The person who operates My Growth Modules is the controller of your personal data. Contact: hello@mygrowthmodules.com (the Netherlands). We do not list a street address here. If you need one for a legal request, ask by email.

2. What we collect

  • Account. Sign-in through Google or Sign in with Apple (name, email, and a Google or Apple user id). We do not receive your Google or Apple password. Apple may share a private relay email instead of your real address. Email-and-password login is not in the app yet.
  • Profile. Display name, photo, optional bio, height, weight, age, sex at birth (for calorie estimates), country, language, units, and (if you add it) your own phone number so friends can find you from their contacts. Sex at birth is not shown on your public profile. Your address book is not stored: names and numbers from the phone are checked briefly and then discarded.
  • Photos. Profile photo, optional bodyweight progress photos, optional meal photos you send to MGM AI, and photos you put on custom foods or meals. Cloud copies are stored in private file storage, not on a public website. In the updated Bodyweight photo flow, cloud backup is off until you choose to enable it; existing saved choices remain unchanged. Without cloud backup, progress photos stay on the device. When you take a photo inside MGM, the original is saved to your device's Photos library by default and MGM uses a separate processed copy inside the app. You can turn saving originals off in General Settings. A photo you select from the library is not added to the library again.
  • Module logs. Food and water logs, custom foods and meals, weight logs, optional body-fat percentage, fat mass, explicitly labelled muscle or skeletal-muscle mass, fat-free mass and total body water with its reported unit, and optional body-circumference measurements that you enter. Body measurements are stored with the weight log and do not change your calorie plan. Step totals and manual step entries, and goals you set.
  • Workout (where enabled). Fitness sessions, exercise names, sets, weights, repetitions, durations, rest settings, optional effort ratings and notes, and reusable routines. These records are private to your account and synchronize with our database. Your device retains account-separated offline records across sign-out so interrupted synchronization does not lose a training. Removing an item hides it while retaining a deletion marker and its saved record until account deletion. Workout does not send training records to AI, publish them to friends or write workouts to Apple Health.
  • Health. During setup you can connect Apple Health so MGM can fill date of birth, sex, height and weight, count steps, and read individual workout records such as sport, source, time, duration, distance and active energy. You can also connect later in MGM Settings. MGM does not import Apple's general active-energy total. It queries steps in an exact workout interval to avoid counting the same movement twice. With optional Health permission, MGM can also read available workout-associated heart-rate summaries (average, minimum and maximum), ascent and descent, and sport-specific distance, speed, cycling power or cadence, and swimming strokes. These compact summaries are stored privately with the Nutrition log and synced to your account. MGM does not import raw heart-rate samples or GPS routes for this feature. Declining these optional measurements does not stop normal workout logging, and they are not automatically sent to AI. Imported workout rows remain visible in Nutrition; a setting controls whether their energy changes calorie and macro targets. With Nutrition connected, a new saved workout can produce a local device notification with its sport and calorie value; estimates and unavailable energy are identified. This notification is on by default when device notification permission is granted and can be turned off in MGM notification settings. Depending on your device notification settings, that information may appear on the lock screen. Supported app builds may receive workout updates in the background using a workout observer. When connected and Steps is enabled, supported iPhones may periodically refresh recent step totals while MGM is in the background and sync them to your account. iOS and Apple Watch synchronization control delivery; force-quitting MGM can stop background updates. We do not read Health history until you connect in the app. Health data is not used for advertising. To change access, use Apple Health: profile → Privacy → Apps → MGM.
  • Friends and messages. Friend requests, who you share with, and chats (including support chat with the operator). Where available, you can send photos: we resize them and store them privately for the participants in that conversation. We store delivery acknowledgements and a temporary typing indicator, which expires after six seconds and contains no draft text. Your device keeps pending messages and prepared photos so interrupted sends can be retried with the same message identifier. A bounded set of recent chat text and sent or downloaded chat photos is cached on your device, separately for each account and conversation, so an existing chat can open offline after restarting the app. The text cache does not contain photo bytes. Deleting a chat on that device removes its local text and photo cache; permanently deleting your account removes that account's cached chat text, photos, pending outbox records and prepared originals. Ordinary sign-out keeps the account-separated cache for a later sign-in. You can delete a 1:1 chat from your inbox: it stays for the other person until they delete it too. When both of you have deleted it, we remove its messages from our servers. A new message from them can put it back on your list. Unfriend removes the chat for both of you (support chat stays). Photo files may remain privately stored under the sender's account until account deletion; once the chat is removed, the former recipient cannot request new access to those files. Previously downloaded copies on a recipient's device cannot be recalled.
  • Plans. Whether your plan is Basic, a paid Apple subscription, or a complimentary grant, plus related dates. For subscription accounting we retain verified Apple transaction identifiers, product, purchase and expiry dates, price, currency, refund status and test/production environment. Apple processes payments. We do not store your card number.
  • Service statistics. We record an hourly activity marker while you use the app to count unique active accounts, plus anonymous changes in the total number of accounts. We also record which modules and the leaderboard are viewed and whether a goal is active. We combine these markers with action metadata (such as creating a meal or manual product, importing steps, adding a weight log, using AI or sending a message) to calculate percentages of unique active users. Activity markers are linked to your account and removed when that account is deleted. These statistics contain no diary contents or messages and are available only to the operator.
  • MGM AI (meal estimate). If you use it, a photo and/or text of a meal is sent to our servers and to Google Gemini to estimate nutrition. We store that conversation (your messages, meal photos you send, and the estimate cards) so you can reopen it after a crash or leaving the screen. Your device also keeps a bounded, account- and conversation-specific history cache for offline reopening. Compact photo copies can remain on your device after both failed and successful analyses, linked to the exact account, conversation and photo message, so you can enlarge or explicitly analyse the same photo again after reopening. The local cache of photos with an existing server copy is bounded and does not create additional server copies. Older cloud-backed local copies may be cleared to limit device storage; the existing private server photo can then be downloaded again. A successful local photo whose upload failed remains available as the only app copy until you delete its conversation or account. Conversation deletion or permanent account deletion removes the corresponding local cache. Ordinary sign-out preserves account-separated copies for a later sign-in. You can delete a conversation; that removes the conversation and its photos from our servers immediately. When you log a meal made from a photo, a separate private copy of that photo is kept with the meal log and synced to your account; deleting the AI conversation does not delete your meal log or its photo. We also store the resulting foods in your log, a private AI memory for you, and usage/cost counters. Ungrounded product names may be stored in an aggregated catalog-gap list that is not tied to your identity. Product scanning uses a barcode or supported product QR identifier to search the catalog. For an unknown code, you can choose to take a photo and send it to MGM AI; the code stays with your private conversation. After a nutrition estimate is available, the code and estimated product name may be copied to an operator-only catalog-gap list without your account identifier, photo or conversation. This list supports manual catalog review and does not automatically publish your photo or create a catalog product.
  • Bodyweight report chat. A measurement-report photo you choose is sent to our server and Google Gemini to read the values. You can crop names and identifiers to share fewer personal details. The report photo, conversation and draft values are saved in your private MGM AI chat so you can reopen them, ask questions and correct the result. A local copy supports reopening photos on your device; the original in your photo library remains yours. Follow-up requests use your current message and the latest report draft; the selected report photo is read again only when you explicitly ask to reanalyse it. Values become a Bodyweight log only when you choose Log. If that saved proposal used a report photo, an independent copy is attached to the log as its source. This attachment starts private and follows your existing Bodyweight photo-backup choice and protection. You can remove it using the log's photo controls without removing the chat photo. Deleting the chat does not remove a weight log or source photo you explicitly saved. Report chats use your existing MGM AI allowance; usage accounting does not contain the photo or measurement values.
  • Device. Data stored on your phone so the app works offline. If the app fails we may send a crash report with error text, stack, screen name, app version/build, operating system/version, session times and, if signed in, a user id. A short local record of up to 20 predefined technical events (such as opening a screen or swiping a Steps fact) helps explain an unexpected exit at the next start. These events contain no typed text, meal contents, photos, chat messages or Health measurements. Known secret patterns are stripped from errors. Similar reports are grouped to count occurrences and affected signed-in users; grouping by the last screen/action does not prove the cause. Crash email summaries and diagnostic attachments go to the operator only.
  • Notifications. Reminder preferences and scheduled reminders stay on your device. Permission is asked on the phone. When a friend messages you, we store a device push token and send a lock-screen notification through Expo, then Apple or Google. The lock-screen text may include up to 80 characters of that message. When an AI answer finishes while you are outside that conversation, the app can also show a local notification with up to 80 characters of the answer. Supported background photo requests instead use a generic completion push through Expo in the request's selected app language. This push contains the existing device token and a general ready message, without the photo, answer, measurements, account identifier or conversation identifier. Tapping it opens the current signed-in account's AI inbox. Account-specific read markers stay in the local AI history cache. Turning Chat off in Notifications, signing out, or deleting your account stops those notifications. We do not send marketing push.
  • Technical logs. Our hosts (and GitHub, which serves this page) may log IP address, browser, and time when you open the app or this page, to keep the service running and secure.

In app versions that support background AI photo processing, we keep an account-owned processing record with the existing private photo reference, request settings, status and answer. An accepted request can continue on our server when you leave the app; reopening reads the saved result instead of automatically repeating a paid analysis. The record follows its source conversation and account deletion. Stop cancels pending work; it does not remove an answer already saved or a log you explicitly created.

3. Why we use it

To run your account, sync across devices, show friends what you choose to share, provide paid or complimentary plans, estimate meals you ask MGM AI to read, send the reminders you enable, ping you when a friend writes, and keep the app reliable. Legal bases under GDPR include the contract (providing MGM), consent (Health, camera, photo library, notifications, optional contacts), and legitimate interests (security, crash diagnosis, aggregated catalog gaps, operating the service). We also keep operational request and model-attempt metadata (model, token counts, estimated cost, outcome and conversation identifier) to measure service costs and enforce weekly usage limits. These records contain no conversation text or photos and are not reset when a chat is deleted.

5. How long we keep it

While your account is open, your logs and profile stay so the app can work. Device push tokens stay until you sign out, turn Chat off (the token remains but chat pings stop), or we delete the account. If you delete your account in Profile Settings you must type deletemygrowth. The account stays recoverable for 30 days (sign in to keep it). During those 30 days you can also type deletenow on the deletion screen to remove the live account and the private copy immediately. If you wait, we delete the live account after 30 days. A private per-account archive may then be kept for up to 180 days so we can restore one person if needed, then it is removed (this archive is not kept after a deletenow delete). Apple subscription records follow Apple’s rules. Crash reports remain available as archived diagnostics after resolution so recurring problems can be investigated. Their account reference is removed when the account is deleted. The operator can remove diagnostic records; resolving a report does not delete it. The short technical event record on your device is replaced by the next session.

AI catalog diagnostics. Where enabled, we keep limited troubleshooting records linking an AI estimate to its catalog search terms, candidate product identifiers, selected products and the identifiers of foods you actually log. The operator can use these links to inspect the existing estimate and matching log entries, investigate mistakes and improve catalog matching. We do not make additional copies of your photos or conversation messages for this purpose. Diagnostic records become inaccessible after 30 days and are removed by a cleanup job running every five minutes. Deleting the associated conversation or account also removes these records; expiry does not delete your diary or original conversation. If sending a diagnostic log link fails, your device may keep only the relevant identifiers for up to 30 days, retry on later use and discard expired identifiers when the app next processes that queue.

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA and UK), you may access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent (for example disconnect Health or turn sharing off). In the app you can change sharing, disconnect Health, and delete your account. There is no in-app download of a data copy. For a copy of your data, or a correction we cannot do in the app, email hello@mygrowthmodules.com. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

7. Children

MGM is intended only for adults aged 18 or older, including existing users. Do not create an account for anyone under 18. We use the date of birth already stored in your MGM profile to check eligibility; an App Store age rating is not a substitute for this check. AI eligibility is checked within MGM, without sending your date of birth to Google for that check.

An age restriction does not automatically erase an existing account or its records. The retention and account-deletion periods above still apply. You may contact hello@mygrowthmodules.com to correct an incorrect date of birth, obtain a copy of your records, or ask for help with an account belonging to someone under 18.

8. Security and international transfers

Cloud data is protected by sign-in and by who you share with. Processors may store data outside your country; they are required to protect it under their contracts with us. No method of transmission is perfectly secure.

Private Bodyweight photos: encryption on your device. We are testing an additional privacy layer for Bodyweight progress photos. It is not yet a guarantee for existing released apps or existing stored photos. In the new photo vault, private cloud backups are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before upload. MGM stores the encrypted photo, but does not store the private key needed to directly view it. Our developers, or someone with access only to our database and file storage, therefore cannot directly view those private encrypted photos. New photos are resized and compressed on the device before encryption, with unnecessary image metadata removed, to reduce storage and transfer size. Existing originals are not replaced by this compression change.

Sharing a photo. When this vault is available, explicitly sharing a progress photo creates a separate, ordinary server copy for accepted friends under your sharing settings. The encrypted private original remains. MGM and its storage administrator can technically access the shared copy. Turning sharing off revokes new access and requests actual deletion of that copy; incomplete deletion is shown as pending. Copies made outside MGM cannot be recalled. Turning backup off keeps photos on the device and removes registered server copies only after confirming a usable local copy.

Optional photo lock and recovery. Photo locking is off by default unless you have already chosen to enable it. You can turn it on to use your device’s biometric authentication or passcode to control viewing of unshared Bodyweight photos in the module. Published photos remain visible without unlocking, including on your profile. Turning this viewing lock off does not turn off private-backup encryption. The private key is kept in Apple Keychain, with iCloud Keychain synchronization where available. Recovery on a replacement device requires the matching key to be available through Apple Keychain, an accessible earlier device or a previously saved recovery code. MGM does not store a plaintext recovery code and cannot restore a genuinely lost key. Older private server photos are converted on the device; the old copy is removed only after the encrypted replacement has been verified and the applicable migration checks have passed. Existing development staging data may be converted under explicit approval without independent recovery verification.

The Bodyweight vault covers private progress photos. Selected development accounts may also use the separate encrypted AI-chat route described above. Profile photos, ordinary Nutrition media and other media retain their existing protection. Device photo libraries, compromised devices and copies made outside MGM are outside this protection. Encryption does not make any system immune to every possible attack.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For a change that really affects you we will try to tell you in the app or by email. If you do not agree, delete your account and stop using MGM.
