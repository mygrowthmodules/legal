Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026. Contact: sam@mygrowthmodules.com

This policy explains what MGM collects, why, and how you can delete it. MGM is a personal growth app (nutrition, bodyweight, steps, friends). It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

1. Who is responsible

The person who operates My Growth Modules is the controller of your personal data. Contact: sam@mygrowthmodules.com (the Netherlands). We do not list a street address here. If you need one for a legal request, ask by email.

2. What we collect

  • Account. Sign-in through Google or Sign in with Apple (name, email, and a Google or Apple user id). We do not receive your Google or Apple password. Apple may share a private relay email instead of your real address. Email-and-password login is not in the app yet.
  • Profile. Display name, photo, optional bio, height, weight, age, sex at birth (for calorie estimates), country, language, units, and (if you add it) your own phone number so friends can find you from their contacts. Sex at birth is not shown on your public profile. Your address book is not stored: names and numbers from the phone are checked briefly and then discarded.
  • Photos. Profile photo, optional bodyweight progress photos, optional meal photos you send to MGM AI, and photos you put on custom foods or meals. Stored in our file storage, not on a public website.
  • Module logs. Food and water logs, custom foods and meals, weight logs, step totals and manual step entries, goals you set.
  • Health. During setup you connect Apple Health so MGM can fill date of birth, sex, height, and weight, and count steps. You can also connect later in MGM Settings. We only request those Health types. We do not read Health history until you connect in the app. Health data is not used for advertising. To change access, use Apple Health: profile → Privacy → Apps → MGM.
  • Friends and messages. Friend requests, who you share with, and chats (including support chat with the operator). Where available, you can send photos: we resize them and store them privately for the participants in that conversation. We store delivery acknowledgements and a temporary typing indicator, which expires after six seconds and contains no draft text. Your device keeps pending messages and prepared photos so interrupted sends can be retried with the same message identifier. Sent and downloaded chat photos are also cached on your device, separately for each account and conversation, so they can appear again without another download after restarting the app. Deleting a chat on that device removes its local chat-photo cache; pending-send originals are kept so interrupted sends are not lost. You can delete a 1:1 chat from your inbox: it stays for the other person until they delete it too. When both of you have deleted it, we remove its messages from our servers. A new message from them can put it back on your list. Unfriend removes the chat for both of you (support chat stays). Photo files may remain privately stored under the sender's account until account deletion; once the chat is removed, the former recipient cannot request new access to those files. Previously downloaded copies on a recipient's device cannot be recalled.
  • Plans. Whether your plan is Basic, a paid Apple subscription, or a complimentary grant, plus related dates. For subscription accounting we retain verified Apple transaction identifiers, product, purchase and expiry dates, price, currency, refund status and test/production environment. Apple processes payments. We do not store your card number.
  • Service statistics. We record an hourly activity marker while you use the app to count unique active accounts, plus anonymous changes in the total number of accounts. We also record which modules and the leaderboard are viewed and whether a goal is active. We combine these markers with action metadata (such as creating a meal or manual product, importing steps, adding a weight log, using AI or sending a message) to calculate percentages of unique active users. Activity markers are linked to your account and removed when that account is deleted. These statistics contain no diary contents or messages and are available only to the operator.
  • MGM AI (meal estimate). If you use it, a photo and/or text of a meal is sent to our servers and to Google Gemini to estimate nutrition. We store that conversation (your messages, meal photos you send, and the estimate cards) so you can reopen it after a crash or leaving the screen. You can delete a conversation; that removes the conversation and its photos from our servers immediately. When you log a meal made from a photo, a separate private copy of that photo is kept with the meal log and synced to your account; deleting the AI conversation does not delete your meal log or its photo. We also store the resulting foods in your log, a private AI memory for you, and usage/cost counters. Ungrounded product names may be stored in an aggregated catalog-gap list that is not tied to your identity.
  • Device. Data stored on your phone so the app works offline. If the app fails we may send a crash report with error text, stack, screen name, app version/build, operating system/version, session times and, if signed in, a user id. A short local record of up to 20 predefined technical events (such as opening a screen or swiping a Steps fact) helps explain an unexpected exit at the next start. These events contain no typed text, meal contents, photos, chat messages or Health measurements. Known secret patterns are stripped from errors. Similar reports are grouped to count occurrences and affected signed-in users; grouping by the last screen/action does not prove the cause. Crash email summaries and diagnostic attachments go to the operator only.
  • Notifications. Reminder preferences and scheduled reminders stay on your device. Permission is asked on the phone. When a friend messages you, we store a device push token and send a lock-screen notification through Expo, then Apple or Google. The lock-screen text may include up to 80 characters of that message. Turning Chat off in Notifications, signing out, or deleting your account stops those notifications. We do not send marketing push.
  • Technical logs. Our hosts (and GitHub, which serves this page) may log IP address, browser, and time when you open the app or this page, to keep the service running and secure.

Adaptive calorie plan. In Suggested mode, MGM can use your profile, real weight trend, confirmed complete food logs, and available activity records to adjust calories and the goal date automatically within plan limits. You can turn this feature off in Nutrition settings. The original goal, latest applied adjustment and complete-day confirmations are stored with your private Nutrition preferences and synced to your account. Weigh-in weekdays and morning reminders remain device-local. This calculation does not send these records to a generative AI provider.

Activity burn in Nutrition. When you use activity logging, your activity name, active duration, optional time interval, intensity, calorie estimate and calculation reference are stored privately and synced to your account. MGM keeps a compact daily step total and its calorie basis; it does not copy a complete timeline of Health samples. Step counts for a selected activity interval are queried to estimate overlap, and the resulting overlap count is saved with that activity. Switching Steps off preserves previously saved days. Optional AI activity search sends only the activity description to Google Gemini, not your weight, Health readings or time interval. Requested activity names and suggested reference codes may be kept in an aggregated improvement list without your account identifier. AI model usage shares the account allowance described below.

3. Why we use it

To run your account, sync across devices, show friends what you choose to share, provide paid or complimentary plans, estimate meals you ask MGM AI to read, send the reminders you enable, ping you when a friend writes, and keep the app reliable. Legal bases under GDPR include the contract (providing MGM), consent (Health, camera, photo library, notifications, optional contacts), and legitimate interests (security, crash diagnosis, aggregated catalog gaps, operating the service). We also keep operational request and model-attempt metadata (model, token counts, estimated cost, outcome and conversation identifier) to measure service costs and enforce weekly usage limits. These records contain no conversation text or photos and are not reset when a chat is deleted.

4. Who can see it

  • Friends. Profile and module tiles follow the sharing switches in the app. Each new bodyweight progress photo starts hidden, even if you already share other photos. Before showing a hidden photo, the app asks you to confirm that friends can see it on your profile. You can hide it again without confirmation. Existing photo choices are preserved. Sex at birth stays off unless you turn it on.
  • The operator. We can access account data to run MGM, help you, stop abuse, and fix bugs (including MGM Stats and the support chat). We do not sell it and we do not use your health or food logs for third-party advertising.
  • Processors. Supabase (hosting, database, file storage, auth). Google (Sign-In; Gemini for MGM AI). Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store, subscriptions, Health, notifications, device services). Expo/EAS (building the iOS app and delivering chat push). GitHub (hosting this Privacy Policy and the Terms page). Email delivery for crash alerts to the operator only. Google’s terms apply to Gemini processing.

5. How long we keep it

While your account is open, your logs and profile stay so the app can work. Device push tokens stay until you sign out, turn Chat off (the token remains but chat pings stop), or we delete the account. If you delete your account in Profile Settings you must type deletemygrowth. The account stays recoverable for 30 days (sign in to keep it). During those 30 days you can also type deletenow on the deletion screen to remove the live account and the private copy immediately. If you wait, we delete the live account after 30 days. A private per-account archive may then be kept for up to 180 days so we can restore one person if needed, then it is removed (this archive is not kept after a deletenow delete). Apple subscription records follow Apple’s rules. Crash reports remain available as archived diagnostics after resolution so recurring problems can be investigated. Their account reference is removed when the account is deleted. The operator can remove diagnostic records; resolving a report does not delete it. The short technical event record on your device is replaced by the next session.

AI catalog diagnostics. Where enabled, we keep limited troubleshooting records linking an AI estimate to its catalog search terms, candidate product identifiers, selected products and the identifiers of foods you actually log. The operator can use these links to inspect the existing estimate and matching log entries, investigate mistakes and improve catalog matching. We do not make additional copies of your photos or conversation messages for this purpose. Diagnostic records become inaccessible after 30 days and are removed by a cleanup job running every five minutes. Deleting the associated conversation or account also removes these records; expiry does not delete your diary or original conversation. If sending a diagnostic log link fails, your device may keep only the relevant identifiers for up to 30 days, retry on later use and discard expired identifiers when the app next processes that queue.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA and UK), you may access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent (for example disconnect Health or turn sharing off). In the app you can change sharing, disconnect Health, and delete your account. There is no in-app download of a data copy. For a copy of your data, or a correction we cannot do in the app, email sam@mygrowthmodules.com. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

7. Children

MGM is not directed at children under 16. The app blocks setup and date-of-birth changes if you are under 16. Do not create an account for a child under 16.

8. Security and international transfers

Cloud data is protected by sign-in and by who you share with. Processors may store data outside your country; they are required to protect it under their contracts with us. No method of transmission is perfectly secure.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For a change that really affects you we will try to tell you in the app or by email. If you do not agree, delete your account and stop using MGM.
