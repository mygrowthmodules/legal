Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026. Contact: sam@mygrowthmodules.com

This policy explains what MGM collects, why, and how you can delete it. MGM is a personal growth app (nutrition, bodyweight, steps, friends). It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

1. Who is responsible

The person who operates My Growth Modules is the controller of your personal data. Contact: sam@mygrowthmodules.com (the Netherlands). We do not list a street address here. If you need one for a legal request, ask by email.

2. What we collect

  • Account. Sign-in through Google (name, email, and a Google user id). We do not receive your Google password. Apple Sign-In and email-and-password login are not in the app yet.
  • Profile. Display name, photo, optional bio, height, weight, age, sex at birth (for calorie estimates), country, language, units, and (if you add it) your own phone number so friends can find you from their contacts. Sex at birth is not shown on your public profile. Your address book is not stored: names and numbers from the phone are checked briefly and then discarded.
  • Photos. Profile photo, optional bodyweight progress photos, optional meal photos you send to MGM AI, and photos you put on custom foods or meals. Stored in our file storage, not on a public website.
  • Module logs. Food and water logs, custom foods and meals, weight logs, step totals and manual step entries, goals you set.
  • Health. If you connect Apple Health, MGM reads step data you allow. We do not read Health history until you connect in the app. Health data is not used for advertising.
  • Friends and messages. Friend requests, who you share with, and chats (including support chat with the operator). You can delete a 1:1 chat from your inbox: it stays for the other person until they delete it too. When both of you have deleted it, we remove it from our servers. A new message from them can put it back on your list. Unfriend removes the chat for both of you (support chat stays).
  • Plans. Whether your plan is Basic, a paid Apple subscription, or a complimentary grant, plus related dates. Apple processes payments. We do not store your card number.
  • MGM AI (meal estimate). If you use it, a photo and/or text of a meal is sent to our servers and to Google Gemini to estimate nutrition. We store that conversation (your messages, meal photos you send, and the estimate cards) so you can reopen it after a crash or leaving the screen. You can delete a conversation; that removes it from our servers immediately, including its photos. We also store the resulting foods in your log, a private AI memory for you, and usage/cost counters. Ungrounded product names may be stored in an aggregated catalog-gap list that is not tied to your identity.
  • Device. Data stored on your phone so the app works offline. If the app fails we may send a crash report (error text, stack, screen name, app version, and if you were signed in a user id). Payment secrets are stripped. Crash mail goes to the operator only.
  • Notifications. Reminder preferences and scheduled reminders stay on your device. Permission is asked on the phone. We do not send marketing push from our servers.
  • Technical logs. Our hosts (and GitHub, which serves this page) may log IP address, browser, and time when you open the app or this page, to keep the service running and secure.

3. Why we use it

To run your account, sync across devices, show friends what you choose to share, provide paid or complimentary plans, estimate meals you ask MGM AI to read, send the reminders you enable, and keep the app reliable. Legal bases under GDPR include the contract (providing MGM), consent (Health, camera, photo library, notifications, optional contacts), and legitimate interests (security, crash diagnosis, aggregated catalog gaps, operating the service).

4. Who can see it

  • Friends. Profile and module tiles follow the sharing switches in the app. Bodyweight photos and sex at birth stay off unless you turn them on.
  • The operator. We can access account data to run MGM, help you, stop abuse, and fix bugs (including MGM Stats and the support chat). We do not sell it and we do not use your health or food logs for third-party advertising.
  • Processors. Supabase (hosting, database, file storage, auth). Google (Sign-In; Gemini for MGM AI). Apple (App Store, subscriptions, Health, notifications, device services). Expo/EAS (building and delivering the iOS app). GitHub (hosting this Privacy Policy and the Terms page). Email delivery for crash alerts to the operator only. Google’s terms apply to Gemini processing.

5. How long we keep it

While your account is open, your logs and profile stay so the app can work. If you delete your account in Profile Settings you must type deletemygrowth. The account stays recoverable for 30 days (sign in to keep it). After that we delete the live account. A private per-account archive may be kept for up to 180 days so we can restore one person if needed, then it is removed. Apple subscription records follow Apple’s rules. Crash reports are kept until we resolve them.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA and UK), you may access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent (for example disconnect Health or turn sharing off). In the app you can change sharing, disconnect Health, and delete your account. For a copy of your data, or a correction we cannot do in the app, email us. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

7. Children

MGM is not directed at children under 16. Do not create an account for a child under 16.

8. Security and international transfers

Cloud data is protected by sign-in and by who you share with. Processors may store data outside your country; they are required to protect it under their contracts with us. No method of transmission is perfectly secure.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. For a change that really affects you we will try to tell you in the app or by email. If you do not agree, delete your account and stop using MGM.
