Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026. Contact: sam@mygrowthmodules.com

This policy explains what MGM collects, why, and how you can delete it. MGM is a personal growth app (nutrition, bodyweight, steps, friends). It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

1. Who is responsible

The operator of My Growth Modules is the controller of your personal data. Questions or requests: sam@mygrowthmodules.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account. Sign-in through Google (name, email, and a Google user id). We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile. Display name, photo, optional bio, height, weight, age, sex at birth (for calorie estimates only), country, units, and (if you add it) your phone number for finding friends from contacts. Sex at birth is not shown on your public profile.
  • Module logs. Food and water logs, custom foods and meals, weight logs and progress photos, step totals and manual step entries, goals you set.
  • Health. If you connect Apple Health, MGM reads step data you allow. We do not read Health history until you connect in the app. Health data is not used for advertising.
  • Friends and messages. Friend requests, who you share with, and chats (including support chat with the developer).
  • Plans. Whether your plan is Basic, a paid Apple subscription, or a complimentary grant, plus related dates. Apple processes payments. We do not store your card number.
  • MGM AI (meal estimate). If you use it, a photo and/or text of a meal is sent to our servers and to Google Gemini to estimate nutrition. We store the resulting foods in your log, a private AI memory for you, and usage/cost counters. Ungrounded product names may be stored in an aggregated catalog-gap list that is not tied to your identity.
  • Device. Data stored on your phone so the app works offline. Crash reports (technical logs, no payment secrets) if the app fails, so we can fix bugs.
  • Notifications. Reminder preferences stay on your device. Permission is asked on the phone.

3. Why we use it

To run your account, sync across devices, show friends what you choose to share, provide paid or complimentary plans, estimate meals you ask MGM AI to read, send the reminders you enable, and keep the app reliable. Legal bases under GDPR include the contract (providing MGM), consent (Health, camera, notifications, optional contacts), and legitimate interests (security, crash diagnosis, aggregated catalog gaps).

4. Who we share with

  • You control friends. Profile and module tiles follow the sharing switches in the app. Bodyweight photos and sex at birth stay off unless you turn them on.
  • Processors. Supabase (hosting, database, file storage, auth). Google (Sign-In; Gemini for MGM AI). Apple (App Store, subscriptions, Health, notifications, crash-adjacent device services). Expo/EAS (building and delivering the iOS app). Email delivery for crash alerts to the operator only.
  • We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your health or food logs for third-party advertising.

5. How long we keep it

While your account is open, your logs and profile stay so the app can work. If you delete your account in Profile Settings you must type deletemygrowth. The account stays recoverable for 30 days (sign in to keep it). After that we delete the live account. A private per-account archive may be kept for up to 180 days so we can restore one person if needed, then it is removed. Apple subscription records follow Apple’s rules. Crash reports are kept until we resolve them.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA and UK), you may access, correct, delete, or export your data, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent (for example disconnect Health or turn sharing off). Start in the app (sharing, disconnect, delete account) or email us. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

7. Children

MGM is not directed at children under 16. Do not create an account for a child under 16.

8. Security and international transfers

Access to your cloud data is limited to your signed-in account (and to people you explicitly share with). Processors may store data outside your country; they are required to protect it under their contracts with us. No method of transmission is perfectly secure.

9. Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page and the date above. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
